# Chilean Labor Law: Ius Variandi and Corporate Legal Advisory

This skill provides the agent with knowledge and guidelines for handling situations involving Chilean Labor Law, specifically regarding contract modifications (`Ius Variandi`) and shift management.

## Key Principles of Ius Variandi (Article 12 of the Chilean Labor Code)

1. **Unilateral Modification**: The employer can unilaterally change the nature of the services or the workplace.
2. **Requirements**: 
   - Operations must be of a similar nature.
   - The new location must be within the same city or locality.
   - **No Detriment (Menoscabo)**: The change must NOT cause socio-economic, moral, or material harm to the worker.
3. **Dispute Resolution**: If a worker feels the change causes detriment, they can file a complaint with the Labor Inspectorate (Dirección del Trabajo). 
   - A non-compliant worker without a formal complaint may be considered in breach of contract.
4. **Shift Rotations (Mining Sector focus)**: Permanent shift changes (e.g., from an 8x6 to a 5x2 schedule) generally require mutual agreement between the employer and employee, especially if it affects the essential nature of the contract.

## Corporate Legal Advisory Guidelines

When acting as an advisor or handling HR systems (like `Control-doc`):
- **Contract Analysis**: Always cross-reference shift types and job positions against the executed contracts and annexes.
- **Mutual Agreement Logs**: For major shift or location changes, ensure the system records mutual agreement (Anexo de Contrato) securely to prevent labor disputes.
- **Nationality Considerations**: Be mindful of differences in contractual requirements for national vs. foreign workers (e.g., RUT vs. Passport).
- **Compliance**: Follow guidelines typically established by leading firms (like Lizama Abogados) balancing corporate productivity and stringent employee statutory protections.

## How to use this skill
- When requested to analyze contracts or shift changes, consider if the change falls under the legal limit of *Ius Variandi*.
- If a shift modification negatively impacts the worker's rest or salary scheme, flag it as requiring a formal *Anexo de Contrato*.
- If the user asks for legal advice on labor moves, remind them of the "no menoscabo" (no detriment) rule.
